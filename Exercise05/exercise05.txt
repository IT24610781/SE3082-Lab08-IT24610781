3. EXPLANATION
-----------------------
Runs used two processes on Lab03. Mismatch versions change receive source to rank 1 while messages originate from rank 0. Rank 1 waits for a nonexistent self-message. Small MPI_Send calls may return through internal buffering without a matching receive completing. MPI_Bsend reserves 3*(packed_size+MPI_BSEND_OVERHEAD), permits reuse of the variable after returning, and detaches before buffer destruction.
