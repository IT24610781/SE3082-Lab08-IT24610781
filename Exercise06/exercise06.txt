3. EXPLANATION
-----------------------
Workers send counts with MPI_Send and tag 100. Root adds its count and receives size-1 results using MPI_ANY_SOURCE. Trial and timing reductions remain. MPI_ANY_SOURCE belongs in the source argument, not the tag argument (a wording error in the sheet). Reference: https://docs.open-mpi.org/en/main/man-openmpi/man3/MPI_Recv.3.html . Results match Exercise03 with three processes.
