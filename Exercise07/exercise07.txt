3. EXPLANATION
-----------------------
Each worker attaches space for one MPI_LONG_LONG_INT packed message plus MPI_BSEND_OVERHEAD, sends using MPI_Bsend, detaches then frees the buffer. Root retains MPI_ANY_SOURCE and tag 100. Results equal Exercise06. One timing per version is insufficient to claim Bsend is faster.
