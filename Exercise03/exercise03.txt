3. EXPLANATION
-----------------------
Sample x,y in [0,1), count x*x+y*y<=1, estimate pi=4*inside/trials. Each rank has a deterministic pseudorandom state. Trial validation verifies accounting, not exact pi. Fixed process counts repeat estimates; changing process counts changes random streams.
